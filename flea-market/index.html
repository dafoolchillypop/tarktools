Fee = VO·Ti·4PO·Q + VR·Tr·4PR·Q, where VO = base price, VR = your list price, PO = log₁₀(VO/VR), PR = log₁₀(VR/VO). The positive exponent is raised to the 1.08 power. Then the Intel Center / Hideout Management discount is applied. Fees are paid when you list, even if the item never sells. Check the "Fee at current min" figure against the game. If they don't match, the base price is probably off.
